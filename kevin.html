01 / THE CONTEXT

THE PRODUCT DECISION

Study help should know
which material it is using.

THE FRICTION

Study material is often scattered across PDFs, class notes, and separate revision tools. General AI answers can also drift beyond the exact material a student is trying to learn.

THE APPROACH

Kevin organises content by subject and keeps AI study actions tied to the selected sources. Answers carry citations back to the material, while revision tools reuse those same source excerpts.

04 / MY ROLE

AI-ASSISTED DEVELOPMENT

Product thinking
with an AI-assisted build.

Kevin was developed through an AI-assisted workflow using Manus AI. The focus was on shaping the study experience around a clear promise: students should be able to move from their own material to understanding, practice, and review without losing the source trail.

  • Defined the subject-first study workflow and the core product direction.
  • Iterated on the workspace, study tools, and user-facing interactions with Manus AI.
  • Refined source-grounded answers, citation handling, and the revision loop.
  • Used a relational data model to keep study content and progress organised per user.
Current scope: The repository includes subject workspaces, source ingestion, grounded answers, summaries, flashcards with scheduled review, quizzes, exports, exam dates, and study-progress summaries.
05 / ENGINEERING NOTES

THE CHALLENGE

Make the source
part of the answer.

Study material is not always clean selectable text. Some PDFs are scanned, and even after extraction, an answer is only useful if its supporting material can be checked.

  • Challenge: Turn text-based and scanned PDFs into bounded, retrievable source chunks while preserving page references.
  • Decision: Extract text first, OCR pages that lack usable text, split content into overlapping chunks, and resolve generated citation IDs only against the excerpts supplied to the model.
  • Learning: Source-grounded AI needs a retrieval and citation pipeline around the model. When relevant material is missing, saying so is better than filling the gap with an unsupported answer.
